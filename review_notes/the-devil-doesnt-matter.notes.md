# Notes: the-devil-doesnt-matter.md (filename kept for URL stability)

Thesis: The slogan that the devil has lost his place is true in what it says about his accusing, cancelled claim and broken authority, but Scripture states it more carefully (he is a creature on a chain who is still dangerous), and the besieged believer needs that fuller truth.
Volta: "What He Lost and What He Kept" (about 35 percent), then limits, African and Reformed readings, and pastoral help.
Alternative title: Does the Devil Still Have a Seat
Frontmatter: ESV for the epigraph; title and filename unchanged.

## Corrections to the source
Original claimed the devil "forfeited his matter hood" and cannot share the believer's space; Eph 6:12 puts the powers in the heavenly places too, and a spirit is not matter. Isaiah 14 and Ezekiel 28 are not safe foundations. "I saw" is not a simple completed past in the Greek (imperfect with an aorist participle). The unnamed pastor's quote is unverified; presented as untraced.
