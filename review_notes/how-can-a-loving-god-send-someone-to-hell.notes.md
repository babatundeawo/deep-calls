# Notes: how-can-a-loving-god-send-someone-to-hell.md
Word count: 5281 body (original 3084). Filename kept for URL stability. Lint PASSED; REVIEW lines (question marks, contractions) kept knowingly: questions are mostly quoted or posed by the two askers.
Thesis: A loving God and a real hell are compatible once we ask who does the sending, read the Bible's own vocabulary, hear every Christian view in its best form, and refuse to pronounce on any individual dead.
Volta: the move from the traditional case to the four positions and then to justice and freedom (about 55 to 65 percent).
Alternative title: Does a Loving God Send People to Hell?
