# Notes: look-or-see.md (second pass)
Word count: about 6,150 body (original 4,411; first pass 3,524). File name kept for URL stability. Lint: PASSED, no dashes. REVIEW kept: semicolons are in the References lists.
Thesis: Two people can look at the same hill, scan or slide and see different realities, and Scripture says the difference is a sight only God gives, which makes apologetics a form of intercession as well as argument.
Volta: "The Disciple Who Saw Only Defeat" into "One Hill and Several Verdicts" (about 55 percent). Alternative title: Open My Eyes.
