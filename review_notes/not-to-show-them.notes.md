# Notes: not-to-show-them
Word count: about 4440 body words (original 3004). Filename kept for URL stability.
Thesis: A prayer to be raised above those who shamed us is legitimate only when it hands the case to God and asks for elevation to serve others, as Joseph, David and Mordecai show, and as Romans 12 and Matthew 5:44 require.
Volta: "Esther and the Man on the Horse" / "What Jesus and Paul Ask of Us" (about 70 percent), after the steelman at about 25 percent.
Alternative title: Raise Me So They Can Be Fed.
